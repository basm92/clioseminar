> Historically informed economics, or economically informed historical research;
> preferably both.

The Cliometrics Seminar is a new seminar series in Utrecht, bringing together
researchers working at the intersection of economics and history. We meet about
eight times per academic year, on the last Monday of the month, from 13:00 to
14:15.

Sessions take place in **Utrecht city centre, within walking distance of Utrecht
Centraal railway station** — close enough that a seminar can be an afternoon
trip rather than a whole day away from home.

Papers should be substantial, but they can be early stage. By "early stage" we
mean in the economics sense: there should be a serious paper, with serious work
behind it. We also hope presenters come open to constructive discussion, rather
than to use the seminar as practice for defending the paper.

## Ideas for the series

Depending on interest, we can decide to devote specific slots to really
early-stage research or research proposals, with several shorter presentations
per seminar slot. This should not be done before the series is up and running,
and before we have been able to measure interest.

Similarly, we can devote specific slots to reading group-style discussions of
important books or papers in the field. However, it might be better to leave
this up to individual initiative of seminar participants.
