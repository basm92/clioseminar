title: "Apply"
---

We are always looking for papers for the Cliometrics Seminar, and we would be
glad to hear from you — whether you are well established in the field or just
starting out.

There is no form to fill in. To propose a paper, send an email to
[clioseminar@gmail.com](mailto:clioseminar@gmail.com) with:

- the **title** of the paper;
- an **abstract**;
- an indication of your **availability** — which of the
  [scheduled dates](/) work for you, and which do not;
- the **stage** of the paper: a research proposal, a first draft, a working
  paper, or something close to submission.

That last point matters to us. Early-stage work is very welcome here. By "early
stage" we mean it in the economics sense: there should be a serious paper, with
serious work behind it. What we ask is that you come open to constructive
discussion, rather than to use the seminar as practice for defending the paper.

We will get back to you with a slot and confirm the date well in advance.
Sessions run from 13:00 to 14:15 in Utrecht city centre, within walking distance
of Utrecht Centraal railway station.

If you are unsure whether your work fits, or you would simply like to come along
first, you are very welcome to do so — [get in touch](/contact/) and we will add
you to the mailing list.
